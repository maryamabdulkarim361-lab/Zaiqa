Zaiqa · Hologram Pyramid Mode

Turn any dish video into a floating hologram

Pick a short rotating video of a dish. This screen will split it into four mirrored views — place a hologram pyramid on the center of your screen and the dish appears to float inside it.

or try a sample
Don't have a rotating dish video yet? — Open index.html, tap a "Live 3D" dish so it auto-rotates, then screen-record it for 5–10 seconds. Or, if you scan dishes with Scaniverse/Polycam, most of those apps can export a turntable video directly — no screen recording needed.
Prototype note — this is the physical hologram-pyramid version. It plays a pre-recorded video, not a live interactive 3D model.
